How It Works

GrowMore takes you from soil test to fertiliser plan in a structured three-step workflow. Every calculation is based on your actual soil test data and farm parameters — no guesswork, no sales influence.

Phase 1

Set up your farm

Create your farm profile

Start by entering your farm name, region, and type — Dairy or Drystock. The app uses this to select the right nutrient targets and default stocking parameters for your system. Takes about two minutes the first time; once saved it's there every time you open the app.

Add your blocks

A block is any area of your farm you want to manage separately — different paddocks, soil types, or management zones. For each block, enter the name, size in hectares, and soil type (Sedimentary, Pumice, Volcanic Ash, or Peat).

You can run fertiliser applications across multiple blocks of the same soil type, or manage them individually. The app stores all your blocks and their historical soil tests so you build up a picture of how your farm's fertility changes over time.

Phase 2

Enter your soil test

Load your results

The app works with results from any accredited NZ lab — Eurofins, Hill Labs, PAL, or others. You can use your most recent test, select multiple tests for weighted averaging across large blocks, or enter results manually. Camera scanning and direct lab import are coming soon.

The app uses 12 inputs: Olsen P, K, Ca, Mg, Na, pH, Sulphate S (SO4-S), Extractable Organic Sulphur (EOS), CEC, Anion Storage Capacity (ASC), and Volume Weight.

See where you stand

Once your results are loaded, the app generates a radar chart showing your actual nutrient levels against the ideal range for your soil and farm type. A colour-coded bar for each nutrient shows exactly how far above or below target you are.

A written summary explains what the numbers mean in plain language — which nutrients need building, which are adequate, and what the implications are for this season's programme.

pH is read from the same test. Where your soil sits below target, the app works out an Ag-Lime rate in tonnes per hectare from your pH.

Phase 3

Compare and choose

Every option, ranked by cost

The app calculates the custom blend needed to meet your nutrient targets, then prices it across every participating supplier — Ballance, Ravensdown, Dickie Direct, Marnco and more. Results are ranked by estimated on-ground cost, including cartage, blending and spreading.

The lowest on-ground cost option is highlighted as the recommended choice. You can toggle between on-ground and ex-store pricing depending on what's relevant to you.

Lime sits on its own tab beside the supply options, showing the rate your pH calls for. In this release it's a recommendation only — it isn't added to the order with the blend.

Fine-tune your application

Before committing, adjust your application parameters using the Fine Tune controls:

  • Nitrogen rate — include or exclude N, choose inhibitor options
  • Capital vs maintenance split — how aggressively to build soil fertility
  • Trace elements — based on your herbage test results
  • Product preference — conventional, regenerative, or certified organic

The product preference switch is the one worth playing with. It changes which products the app is allowed to use, so you can price a conventional programme, a regenerative one and a certified organic one against the same soil test and see what each actually costs.

Every adjustment recalculates the blend and pricing instantly across all suppliers.

Review the full breakdown

Tap any supplier to see the detailed blend — exactly which products are included, rates per hectare, nutrient delivery by element, and the full cost breakdown with cartage, blending, and spreading shown separately.

If the supplier participates in the platform you can place your order directly through the app. For suppliers not yet on the platform, the plan and pricing are still shown — you place the order with them directly.

One workflow. Full visibility. Clear decisions.

From soil test to signed-off fertiliser plan, GrowMore gives you the information to make a confident call — based on what your soil actually needs.
