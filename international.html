International

GrowMore is currently calibrated for New Zealand pastoral farming conditions. International expansion is planned.

Why NZ first

New Zealand has decades of rigorous, locally calibrated soil science underpinning its pastoral industry. The nutrient targets, lab extract methods, and calibration data the app relies on are NZ-specific. Getting this right in one market before expanding into others ensures the science behind the recommendation is sound.

Interest from other markets

The core problem — farmers relying on supplier-employed advisors for nutrient recommendations, without access to independent science or price comparison — is not unique to New Zealand. Pastoral farming systems in Australia, Ireland, the UK, and parts of South America face similar dynamics.

If you're from an international market and want to explore bringing GrowMore to your region, get in touch.
